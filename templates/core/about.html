How It Began

It started with families who refused to let any brother stand alone

Living and working across the municipalities and towns of Ghana's Eastern Region—from Koforidua to Suhum, Nsawam, Nkawkaw, and Somanya—people of Kabye lineage carried hard work in their hands and heritage in their hearts. Yet when sudden hospital emergencies struck, when marriages were solemnized, or when bereavement fell upon a household, families often faced the burden scattered and isolated.

In response, elders, women leaders, and youth organizers convened to establish an enduring institutional home. Rather than relying on informal promises, they ratified the Supreme Branch Charter—anchoring our fraternity on mandatory monthly welfare dues, serialized digital receipts, quarterly civic service, and bi-monthly democratic assemblies.

Constitutional Clarity

Questions members & allies ask before joining

Direct answers drawn straight from our ratified Branch Constitution and Bye-Laws.

Membership is open to all individuals of Kabye heritage by birth, marriage, or legal/cultural affiliation who reside or work within the Eastern Region of Ghana. Applicants submit a formal registration accompanied by the one-time statutory induction fee of GHC 50.00 and are presented at the Ordinary General Assembly.
Every active member contributes a mandatory monthly subscription of GHC 20.00 payable by the last day of each calendar month via our institutional Mobile Money or bank account. Upon payment, the Financial Secretary issues an official serialized digital receipt. Defaulting beyond 3 months triggers a formal arrears notice, and default exceeding 6 months suspends voting and welfare entitlements.
Under Article 5.1, welfare rights vest after 6 continuous months of active membership and uninterrupted dues compliance. Once vested, you are protected across all five statutory packages: Childbirth & Naming, Lawful Marriage, Severe In-Patient Illness, Bereavement Solidarity (member, spouse, child, or biological parent), and Natural Calamity relief.
No single officer can withdraw or spend association funds. All accounts operate under strict dual mandatory signatories (the Branch Chairman and Branch Treasurer), backed by serialized receipt books maintained by the Financial Secretary and regular audits presented openly to the General Assembly.
Ordinary General Assemblies convene bi-monthly on the 1st and 3rd Sundays of every month at 2:00 PM prompt. Under Article 7, deliberations are conducted inclusively in Kabiye, English, Twi, and French so every member participates fully.
