Quick Constitutional Reference

Article 1 • Supremacy & Jurisdiction

Name & Legal Supremacy

Recognized as the United Kabye Association of Ghana, Eastern Region Branch, governed subordinate to the National Constitution.

Article 2 • Governance

Executive Directorate

Elected by universal secret ballot at AGM for a 3-year tenure (max 2 consecutive terms). Two-thirds (2/3) Assembly vote required for impeachment.

Article 3 • Subscriptions

GHC 20.00 Monthly Dues & Sanctions

Payable by the final day of each month. 3 months default triggers written arrears notice; over 6 months suspends voting and welfare entitlements.

Article 4 • Induction

Eligibility & GHC 50.00 Registration

Open to adults (18+) of Kabye heritage by birth, marriage, or affiliation residing/working in the Eastern Region upon payment of GHC 50.00 induction fee.

Article 5 • Statutory Welfare Scheme

6-Month Vesting & 5 Qualifying Welfare Events

Requires 6 continuous months of active registration and financial good standing (max 1 claim per 3-month window; siblings assessed individually). Covers: (1) Childbirth & Naming, (2) Lawful Marriage, (3) Severe Illness / In-Patient Admission, (4) Bereavement (member, spouse, biological child, or parent), and (5) Natural Calamity / Disaster.

Article 6 • Assemblies

1st & 3rd Sundays @ 2:00 PM

Bi-monthly General Assemblies require a 1/3 active member quorum. Policy changes require a 2/3 supermajority.

Article 7 • Languages & Conduct

Multilingual & Platform Discipline

Official media: Kabiye, English, Twi, and French. Progressive discipline: (1) Written Caution, (2) 7-day suspension, (3) Disciplinary Committee trial.

Article 8 • Fiduciary Controls

Dual Signatories & Serialized Receipts

Institutional bank/MoMo accounts require joint Chairman & Treasurer signatures. Audited annually by an independent 3-member Internal Audit Committee.

Articles 9 & 10 • Amendments & CSR

Quarterly Clinics & Orphanage Missions

Bye-laws require 2/3 Assembly vote to amend. Social & Welfare Committee leads quarterly clinic/orphanage visitations with 1-month advance notice.
