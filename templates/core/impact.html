How Missions Are Governed

Structured service, audited down to the last cedi

Under Article 10 of our Branch Constitution, community outreach is never ad-hoc—it is budgeted, executed by designated officers, and verified before the assembly.

01 • Planning

Social & Welfare Committee

Identifies high-need pediatric wards, children's homes, and municipal sanitation zones each quarter and submits itemized supply budgets to the Executive.

02 • Execution

Women's & Youth Organizers

Mobilize branch members across Koforidua, Suhum, Nsawam, and Nkawkaw to deliver relief items in person and lead Saturday civic cleanups.

03 • Disclosure

Dual-Signatory & Audit

All outreach disbursements require joint Chairman and Treasurer authorization, with purchase receipts audited and read at the next Sunday assembly.
